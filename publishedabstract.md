# Exploring the Impact of the 3 Zero Movement in Bangladesh: A Pathway to Sustainable Development
### December 2025
### Conference: 18th International Conference on Engineering & Natural SciencesAt: Kyrenia, TRNC
### Author: Minhajul Abedin

## Abstract
Bangladesh is growing fast in terms of its economy and population, and that's making things tricky. The country has to figure out how to grow without messing things up for the future. They've made headway in some important areas so far, like health and education. But there are still problems like poverty, not enough jobs, and damage to the environment that could cause trouble later on. That's why this research is looking at the 3 Zero Movement, which is all about getting to zero poverty, zero unemployment, and zero carbon emissions, in order to deal with these linked issues. This piece will see how well the current projects under the 3 Zero Movement are doing in Bangladesh. It will use numbers on things like poverty reduction and job creation, mixed with reviews of environmental rules. The point is to see what's missing, what's stopping things from working better, and how the different parts of the movement fit together. The hope is to come up with some advice that can help make things better on a practical level. By checking out the 3 Zero Movement, this research wants to give helpful advice to leaders, aid workers, and researchers. Basically anyone who's helping Bangladesh can build a sustainable future.
